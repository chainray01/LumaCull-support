## <a name="english-version"></a>Privacy Policy (English Version)

**Effective date: November 25, 2026**

### 1. Introduction

Welcome to LumaCull! LumaCull is a macOS photo viewing and culling tool for photographers. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information LumaCull accesses and how it is handled when you use the app.

### 2. Information We Collect

**LumaCull does not collect any personal data.** We do not collect, transmit, or store your information on our servers. Specifically:

- **Your Photos**: LumaCull reads the folders and photos that you choose to open, in order to display them. Your photos are never uploaded, copied to any server, or shared with us or anyone else.
- **Photo Information**: Details shown in the info panel (such as color profile, bit depth, dimensions, file name, and folder) are read from your files locally on your Mac and are not transmitted anywhere.
- **Ratings and Preferences**: Your star ratings, your list of recently opened folders, and app settings are stored locally on your device only.
- **No Tracking**: LumaCull does not include advertising, analytics, or third-party tracking tools, and it does not collect usage data, device identifiers, or IP addresses.

### 3. How We Use Your Information

Because we do not collect your information, we do not use it for any purpose. Data that stays on your device (such as ratings and recent folders) is used solely to provide the app's features.

### 4. File Access and Permissions

LumaCull only accesses files and folders that you explicitly select, by opening them through the app or dragging them into its window. The app uses the permissions granted by macOS for this purpose, and you can manage or revoke access at any time in System Settings. Actions you perform on photos, such as rating or deleting, are carried out locally on your device.

### 5. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties, because we do not collect it. If you use the app's share function, the photos are passed to the macOS sharing service or app you choose, and your use of that service is governed by its own privacy policy.

### 6. Data Security

Your photos and data remain on your device, protected by the security features of macOS. We recommend keeping your Mac up to date and backing up your photos regularly.

### 7. Children's Privacy

LumaCull does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

### 8. Your Rights

Since we do not hold any of your personal data, there is nothing for us to access, correct, or delete on your behalf. You can remove all locally stored app data at any time by deleting the app and its associated data from your Mac.

### 9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date, and we encourage you to review it periodically.

### 10. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at:

Email: [privacy@chainray.com]

## <a name="中文版本"></a>隐私政策 (中文版本)

**生效日期：2026 年 11 月 25 日**

### 1. 简介

欢迎使用 LumaCull！LumaCull 是一款面向摄影师的 macOS 看图与选片工具。我们致力于保护您的隐私。本隐私政策说明您在使用 LumaCull 时，应用会访问哪些信息以及如何处理这些信息。

### 2. 我们收集的信息

**LumaCull 不会收集任何个人数据。** 我们不会收集、传输您的信息，也不会将其存储在我们的服务器上。具体而言：

- **您的照片**：LumaCull 仅读取您主动打开的文件夹和照片，用于显示。您的照片不会被上传、复制到任何服务器，也不会与我们或任何第三方共享。
- **照片信息**：信息面板中显示的内容（如色彩描述、色深、尺寸、文件名和所在文件夹）均在您的 Mac 本地从文件中读取，不会传输到任何地方。
- **评分与偏好设置**：您的星级评分、最近打开的文件夹列表以及应用设置仅保存在您的设备本地。
- **无跟踪**：LumaCull 不包含广告、数据分析或第三方跟踪工具，也不会收集使用数据、设备标识符或 IP 地址。

### 3. 我们如何使用您的信息

由于我们不收集您的信息，因此不会将其用于任何用途。保存在您设备上的数据（如评分和最近打开的文件夹）仅用于提供应用功能。

### 4. 文件访问与权限

LumaCull 只会访问您明确选择的文件和文件夹，即通过应用打开或拖入窗口的内容。应用使用 macOS 授予的权限完成此操作，您可以随时在“系统设置”中管理或撤销访问权限。您对照片执行的操作（如评分、删除）仅在您的设备本地完成。

### 5. 信息共享

我们不会出售、出租或与第三方共享您的信息，因为我们不收集这些信息。如果您使用应用的分享功能，照片会交给您选择的 macOS 分享服务或应用，您对该服务的使用将受其自身隐私政策约束。

### 6. 数据安全

您的照片和数据保存在您的设备上，并受 macOS 安全功能的保护。我们建议您及时更新 Mac 系统，并定期备份照片。

### 7. 儿童隐私

LumaCull 不会有意收集任何人的个人信息，包括儿童。

### 8. 您的权利

由于我们不持有您的任何个人数据，因此没有可供我们代您访问、更正或删除的内容。您可以随时通过从 Mac 上删除应用及其相关数据，清除所有本地保存的应用数据。

### 9. 隐私政策的更改

我们可能会不时更新本隐私政策。任何更改都会发布在本页面并更新生效日期，我们建议您定期查看。

### 10. 联系我们

如果您对本隐私政策有任何疑问，请通过以下方式联系我们：

电子邮件：[privacy@chainray.com]
